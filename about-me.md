# About Chaira Harder

Hello! I'm Chaira (ich heiße Chaira/ฉันชื่อชายร่า), co-founder and CEO of [Asaply](https://asaply.ai/), a New York City startup building a text-first AI assistant for real-world errands. People text asaply to get coffee, lunch, tickets, rides, and pickups handled without downloading an app. I built the first version of Asaply at OpenAI's GPT-5 Startup Hackathon in NYC, where it placed Top 6 of 300 engineers, and I now lead a team of 10.

I'm also a software engineer at UNIS in New York City, where I build internal systems that automate manual workflows. I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Smith College.

Having grown up internationally, I'm committed to developing technology and businesses that uplift communities while bridging cultural divides through greater connectivity. I love working on multidisciplinary teams where I can blend software engineering, venture building, and product strategy.

Outside of my professional projects, I'm an enthusiastic athlete who never turns down an adventure. I recharge through sports, live music, discovering new food spots, and traveling to learn from different cultures.
